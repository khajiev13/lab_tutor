### Important — I1: The integrated page disables follow-up typing throughout an attached response

**Locations:** `frontend/src/features/market-demand/pages/MarketDemandPage.tsx:628` (producer), `:379` and `:395` (pending interval); `components/WorkspaceConversation.tsx:49` and `:189` (consumer); `components/ChatInput.tsx:22` (actual disabled textarea). `handleSubmit` sets `sending=true` before awaiting the entire send/reconciliation and clears it only in `finally`. The page then overrides the conversation's `isRestored` with `stream.isRestored && !sending ...`. The conversation interprets this as `unavailable` and passes `disabled` to the actual textarea. Consequently a teacher cannot type a follow-up while the response is streaming, despite the UI explicitly saying “You can edit or stage a question” and Task 9 requiring preservation of text entered while a reply streams. There is no assertion or controlled barrier establishing that the edit happened while the response remained pending. The existing “retained follow-up” receipt therefore does not resolve this issue.
