Material issue

Evidence dialog loses keyboard focus on close. At 320×667, focus Inspect SQL with keyboard, press Enter, then press Escape. After a 350ms close settle there are zero dialogs, but document.activeElement.tagName === 'BODY' and its aria-label is null. Repeat with Inspect ETL / ELT: the same result. Expected: focus returns to the originating Inspect control so a keyboard teacher retains their place.

This is an observed remaining usability defect. No earlier implementation or review was read, so this recheck cannot establish whether it was newly introduced. No new wrapping or horizontal-layout failure was observed in the scoped checks.
